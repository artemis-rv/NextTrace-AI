# Backend research notes

What the sources say, with dates, and how each fact shaped a decision. Storage-side sources (PostgreSQL, Redis,
PCAP tooling, retention guidance) are in [../database/research.md](../database/research.md) and are not repeated.
Facts from abstracts or secondary write-ups are marked as such. Anything we could not verify is marked
**UNVERIFIED**; nothing below is a claim about this system's performance.

## 1. Evaluation protocol for security ML

| Source | Date | Facts used | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arp et al., *Dos and Don'ts of Machine Learning in Computer Security*, USENIX Security 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09470 | 2022 | Ten pitfalls across the ML workflow; *temporal snooping* (mixing past and present samples) overestimates capability; test data must be split early and isolated; thresholds tuned on the test set are a form of snooping. | `mlcommon/splits.py`: chronological splits with embargo gap, scaler fitted on train only, temperature fitted on validation only, test touched once. |
| El Mahdaouy et al., *Deep Learning for Contextualized NetFlow-Based NIDS: Methods, Data, Evaluation and Deployment*, arXiv 2602.05594 | Feb–Mar 2026 | Per-flow classifiers wrongly assume flow independence in multi-stage campaigns; taxonomy of temporal / graph / multimodal / multi-resolution context; temporal leakage and split problems inflate results; deployment needs streaming state management, bounded memory and latency budgets. | Temporal states as the unit of analysis; bounded sequencer state; honest "context helps when attacks create measurable structure" framing in the decisions doc. |
| Guo et al., *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks*, ICML 2017, https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599 | 2017 | Modern networks are poorly calibrated; temperature scaling (one parameter) calibrates well on most datasets. | `mlcommon/calibration.py`; `DetectionResultV1.calibrated` flag; policy inputs use calibrated probabilities only when the flag is set. |
| Sequence models on CIC-IDS2017, arXiv 2606.11098 | 2026-07 | Window length T ∈ {5,10,20}; T = 20 best or indistinguishable. | `NEXTRACE_SEQUENCE_LENGTH=20` default, marked RESEARCH REQUIRED for our data. |
| Zhao & Hassan, *A First-Principles Evaluation of Graph-Based NIDS*, CCS 2026, arXiv 2609.12263 | 2026-09 | Snapshot window alone causes a mean 38.3 % relative swing in average precision; architectural complexity did not consistently help; an encoder-free baseline ranked first on two datasets. | Window length is stored on every state and configurable; a deterministic baseline exists before any GNN is trusted; the GAT is a template until evaluated under one protocol. |
| Wang et al., *Are We There Yet? Unraveling the State-of-the-Art Graph NIDS*, arXiv 2503.20281 | 2025-03 | Reported GIDS results are hard to reproduce; performance varies with dataset scale, inputs and implementation settings, especially false-positive rates. | Model versions record evaluation split, metrics and code ref; no production performance is claimed. |

## 6. Searches that found nothing usable

* Calibration (temperature scaling, ECE) applied to LSTM-attention NIDS on flows, 2024–2025: not found. Calibration therefore follows the general-purpose method and must be measured on our data.
* Lead time ("minutes ahead") for next-stage prediction: a Sept 2025 LSTM DDoS-forecasting study and a next-tactic preprint on UWF-ZeekData24 exist, but neither states a horizon in the material read. Horizon remains RESEARCH REQUIRED.
* A validated multi-stage pattern library over network-only observations: S-DAPT-2026 (arXiv 2601.06690) was withdrawn by its authors for analysis errors. The seed patterns in `correlation/correlator.py` are illustrative only.
